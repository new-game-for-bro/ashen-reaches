## Title
**Ashen Reaches**

## Premise
The world of **Vael** once burned with the light of the **First Flame**, a power that kept death at bay and the land fertile. Centuries ago the flame began to fade. Kings tried to feed it with armies, then with sacrifices, and the attempt shattered the sky in an event called **the Sundering**. Now the land is a graveyard of ash, fog, and half-dead kingdoms, and the dead do not stay dead.

Players are **Emberbound**: souls pulled back from death by a faint spark of the First Flame. They wake with no memory of how they died, a blade, and a mark burning on their hand. The Emberbound cannot truly die while a bonfire is lit, but each death costs them a little more of who they were.

## The Tiers
The Reaches are layered in **Seven Tiers**, shattered pieces of the old sky-kingdom that fell to the ground and stacked into a mountain of ruins. To end the ash, the Emberbound must climb to the top and reach the **Hearth Throne**, where the First Flame still burns.

1. **Cinderfall Hollow** (starting zone): villages and ruined forest
2. **The Weeping Marsh**: drowned graveyards and fog
3. **Ironvein Deeps**: abandoned mines and forges
4. **The Gilded Ruin**: a decaying royal city
5. **Frostbitten Spires**: frozen monasteries
6. **The Hollow Sky**: floating wreckage and storms
7. **The Hearth Throne**: the final tier

## Key characters
- **Maren Ashvale**, the Keeper of the first bonfire. Gentle, tired, hides that she is the last living priestess of the old flame. She guides new Emberbound and knows more than she says.
- **Captain Vessa Roan**, a hardened veteran Emberbound who leads the Cinder Guard and trains players in sword skills. Believes the climb is the only hope.
- **Tobin the Lamplighter**, a cheerful scavenger who sells supplies and collects rumors. Comic relief with a secret: he is a deserter from the Veil Knights.
- **Gravewarden Ormund**, the first boss. A knight who swore to guard the dead of Cinderfall and was driven mad when the flame faded. A tragic figure: his last words ask the player to bury him properly.
- **The Veil Queen Isolde**, the main antagonist. Once the kingdom's last queen, she discovered that the First Flame feeds on souls. She now harvests the Emberbound through the Veil Knights to keep a false flame alive and "preserve" the world.
- **The Hollow Choir**, voices heard at bonfires. Echoes of the fallen who whisper hints and warnings.

## Story arc (main quests)
**Act 1: Awakening (Cinderfall Hollow)**
1. *Spark in the Dark*: The player wakes at a bonfire beside Maren, learns to fight, and receives the mark.
2. *The Cinder Guard*: Captain Vessa tests the player against Ashen Husks and sends them to clear the road.
3. *Lamps in the Fog*: Tobin asks the player to relight three lamp-shrines, which reveals the first glimpse of the Choir.
4. *The Hollow Crypt*: The dead of the crypt are rising. The player descends, fights through the dungeon, and defeats **Gravewarden Ormund**. Ormund drops the **Tier Key** that opens the way to the Weeping Marsh.
5. *A Burden of Ash*: Maren reveals the truth about the Emberbound and the First Flame, and gives the player a choice: carry the flame upward, or let it rest.

**Act 2 onward (outline for future updates)**
- The Veil Knights begin hunting players. Isolde's agents appear in every tier.
- Each tier boss guards a **Flame Shard**. Collecting them reveals that Isolde's plan feeds on the Emberbound souls.
- Vessa is captured. Tobin's deserter past is exposed and he must pick a side.
- Final choice at the Hearth Throne: **rekindle** the First Flame (the world heals, the Emberbound finally die), **claim** it (the player becomes the new Flame, ruling an immortal ashen world), or **extinguish** it (end the cycle of ash, and the world moves on without magic).

## Tone and themes
Bleak but not hopeless. Sacrifice, memory, the cost of clinging to life, and small acts of kindness in a dying world. Dialogue is sparse and weary, with occasional dark humor from Tobin.
